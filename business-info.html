3. Service Description

What BlockMyCard Is

BlockMyCard is a digital helper application that allows Indian cardholders to:

  • Store bank names, card types, and last 4 digits of their cards
  • Save bank card-blocking helpline numbers and procedures
  • Add a trusted family contact for emergencies
  • Quickly access card details when a card is lost or stolen

What BlockMyCard Is NOT

BlockMyCard is NOT a bank and cannot:

  • Block your card directly
  • Access or operate your card
  • Accept deposits or process financial transactions
  • Provide financial advice
  • Guarantee card blocking success
Important: Your bank has the sole responsibility and authority to block your card. BlockMyCard is only a reference tool to help you quickly reach your bank's helpline.

6. Your Consumer Rights

As a consumer using BlockMyCard, you have rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019:

Right to Grievance Redressal: You can file a complaint with BlockMyCard's Grievance Officer or with a Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission if you believe we have provided deficient service.
Right to File a Complaint: File a complaint with your District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission for claims up to ₹1 crore, State Commission for ₹1-10 crore, and National Commission for claims above ₹10 crore.
Right to Compensation: You can claim compensation for deficient service, including damages, loss of opportunity, and mental agony (as determined by the Commission).

How to File a Consumer Complaint

  1. First, contact BlockMyCard: Use our Grievance Redressal Policy (see Section 5) to file a complaint with us. We aim to resolve within 30 days.
  2. If not resolved, file with District Consumer Commission:
    • Visit consumerhelpline.gov.in
    • Find your district's commission
    • Download the complaint form
    • Submit with supporting documents and fee (₹100-500)
  3. State/National Commission: If claim exceeds ₹1 crore, file with higher commission.

8. Security & Safety Measures

What We Do

  • Encrypt all data in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Implement access controls and multi-factor authentication
  • Monitor for suspicious activity 24/7
  • Conduct annual third-party security audits

What You Should Do

  • Keep your phone secure (use PIN/biometric lock)
  • Never share your login OTP with anyone
  • Report suspicious activity immediately to support@blockmycard.in
