Refund & Cancellation Policy

BlockMyCard.in | Vertical Infinity Pvt. Ltd.

Last Updated: 30 July 2026

Our Refund Guarantee

🛡️ 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you purchase any paid features from BlockMyCard and are not satisfied, we offer a full refund within 7 days of your purchase.

No questions asked. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if:

  • You purchased a paid feature and have not used it (or used it minimally)
  • The feature did not work as advertised
  • You are unsatisfied with the service quality
  • Your refund request is made within 7 days of purchase
  • You are the account holder or authorized payment method owner

Refund Process

Step 1: Request a Refund

Email your refund request to support@blockmycard.in

Include:

  • Your registered email address and mobile number
  • Order/Transaction ID
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for refund request
  • Your preferred refund method

Step 2: Review

We will review your request within 2 business days and confirm eligibility via email.

Step 3: Processing

If approved, we will initiate the refund within 5 business days.

Step 4: Receipt

Depending on your payment method, the refund will appear in your account:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 3-5 business days
  • Digital Wallets: 1-3 business days
  • Bank Transfer: 2-5 business days
  • UPI: 1-2 business days

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be provided if:

  • The service was used extensively (more than 7 days of active usage)
  • You request a refund more than 7 days after purchase
  • You purchased the service knowing it was final sale
  • The refund request is due to user error or misunderstanding
  • You have already received another refund for the same purchase

Refund for Service Failure

If BlockMyCard's service fails and causes you financial loss or damages, you may request compensation:

  • Compensation is capped at your actual documented loss or ₹10,000, whichever is lower
  • File a complaint within 30 days of the incident
  • Provide documentation of the service failure and resulting loss
  • We will investigate and respond within 15 days

Subscription Cancellation

If you have an active paid subscription:

  • You can cancel at any time from your account settings
  • Cancellation is effective immediately
  • No refund for the current billing cycle (unless requested within 7 days)
  • You will receive a cancellation confirmation via email
  • For monthly subscriptions, you will not be charged for the next cycle

Chargeback Policy

If you dispute a charge through your bank (chargeback):

  • We will review the chargeback claim
  • We may request evidence of the dispute from your bank
  • If the chargeback is upheld against us, we may suspend or terminate your account
  • Please contact us first via email before filing a chargeback

Payment Methods Accepted

BlockMyCard accepts payment through multiple gateways:

  • Razorpay (Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Wallets, Bank Transfer)
  • Cashfree (Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking)
  • Easebuzz (Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Wallets)

All payments are processed by third-party payment gateways. BlockMyCard does not store full card details.
