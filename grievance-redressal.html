Grievance Redressal Policy

BlockMyCard.in | Vertical Infinity Pvt. Ltd.

Effective Date: 30 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Grievance Redressal Policy ("Policy") sets out BlockMyCard's procedure for receiving, acknowledging, investigating, and resolving complaints from users (consumers) under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

BlockMyCard is committed to:

  • Acknowledging all complaints within 48 hours
  • Investigating complaints fairly and transparently
  • Resolving complaints within 30 days
  • Providing escalation for unresolved grievances
  • Protecting your rights as a consumer

2. What Is a Consumer Grievance?

A complaint qualifies as a consumer grievance if:

  • You have purchased or used BlockMyCard services
  • You believe BlockMyCard has provided deficient service or caused loss/damage
  • The grievance relates to breach of these Terms of Service or Privacy Policy

3. How to File a Complaint

You can file a consumer complaint through any of these channels:

3.1 Email (Recommended)

Send to: support@blockmycard.in

Subject line: "CONSUMER COMPLAINT: [Brief description]"

Include:

  • Your registered mobile number and email
  • Date and time of the incident
  • Detailed description of the problem
  • Screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
  • What resolution you are seeking

Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

📋 Complaint Acknowledgment

Within 48 hours of receiving your complaint, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt of your complaint
  • Provide you with a complaint ticket number
  • Explain the next steps in the resolution process

⏱️ Complaint Resolution

Within 30 days of filing, we will:

  • Complete our investigation
  • Provide you with a resolution (see Resolution Types below)
  • If we cannot resolve, explain why and provide escalation details

Escalation Process

Level 1 (Support Team): Initial complaint handling (0-7 days)

Level 2 (Grievance Officer): If unresolved after 7 days or for complex cases, escalate to Sunil Patel, Grievance Officer

Level 3 (Consumer Commission): If not satisfied after 30 days, you can file with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

Your Rights as a Consumer

Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Right to lodge a complaint for deficient service
  • Right to seek compensation for damages
  • Right to approach the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
  • Right to file with the District, State, or National Consumer Commission depending on claim value
